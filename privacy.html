Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

In short: Stop All Scripts does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Your list of websites stays in Safari on your own device.

Who we are

Stop All Scripts for Safari is published by Dmitry Skorinko, an individual developer, through the App Store.

What the extension does on web pages

On the websites where you turn JavaScript off, Stop All Scripts tells Safari not to run the page’s scripts, before the page starts. On those pages it also shows what the website provides for browsers without JavaScript, loads pictures that would wait for a script, and counts the scripts it stopped, so it can show you the number. It doesn’t keep any page content, doesn’t record which pages you visit, and has no access to your browsing history.

It needs access to the websites you visit so it can stop scripts before the first one runs. You decide where it runs in Safari’s settings.

What is stored

The list of websites where you turned JavaScript off (or, in the other mode, the websites you trust), with the choices you made for each, such as blocking frames or letting JavaScript run until a set time, and your settings. This is kept in Safari’s storage for the extension, on your device.

Nothing leaves your device unless you export the list yourself. Removing the app removes everything it stored.

Network requests

Stop All Scripts makes no requests to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code.

Purchases

Stop All Scripts is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

Stop All Scripts collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
